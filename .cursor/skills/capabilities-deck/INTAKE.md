# Capabilities Deck — Adaptive Intake

Use this question bank selectively. Inspect the operator's files and the brand's
public materials first, then ask only what remains unknown. Never make a PLX user
translate their opportunity into `MicrositeSpec` fields.

## Interview rules

- Ask in short rounds of no more than five questions.
- Lead with choices when the user may not know the design vocabulary.
- Accept files, links, notes, transcripts, and rough ideas as valid answers.
- Summarize what was learned after each round and identify only material gaps.
- Start research and drafting once the minimum brief is ready; do not wait for
  every optional answer.
- Label unresolved facts `Unknown — customer confirmation required`.

## Round 1 — The meeting

Required:

1. What is the opportunity, brand, and customer relationship today?
2. Who will attend, what are their roles, and when is the meeting?
3. What should they approve, share, or do next?
4. What source material already exists: deck, brief, email thread, product list,
   quote, transcript, brand URL, or benchmark?
5. Is the material confidential, and may PLX reference the brand's public
   website and product imagery in a confidential proposal?

Output:

- audience and meeting context
- one-sentence desired outcome
- source inventory
- confidentiality boundary

## Round 2 — The commercial lanes

Ask each lane independently. Remove lanes that are not relevant.

### Existing-business quote

- Which current SKUs or categories should PLX quote?
- Are current annual volumes, typical order quantities, seasonality, pack sizes,
  and target markets known?
- Who supplies packaging and key raw materials?
- What currency, freight basis, payment terms, and quote deadline apply?

### Technical transfer / second source

- Is PLX being considered as a replacement, second source, overflow source, or
  innovation-only partner?
- What may the customer disclose: formula, BOM, process, specifications, testing,
  packaging drawings, benchmark samples, and stability history?
- Is an NDA in place? Who owns or licenses relevant formula/process IP?
- What equivalence and qualification criteria would define success?

### New concepts / samples

- Which concepts should be approved, revised, or declined?
- Is the meeting ask permission to brief, formulate, sample, quote, or launch?
- How many sample programs should be proposed?

Output:

- decision map with one CTA per lane
- quote-readiness status
- missing-information register

## Round 3 — Each concept

Ask only for concepts that remain in scope:

1. Consumer, use moment, and role in the current routine
2. Product form, texture, appearance, fragrance, dosage, and pack direction
3. Mandatory ingredients, excluded ingredients, and benchmark products
4. Intended markets, regulatory category, and permitted claims
5. Sample quantity, evaluation criteria, and decision owner

If answers are unavailable, draft an explicit development direction. Never
present it as an approved formula, finished-product claim, or committed package.

Output:

- one governed concept brief per approved direction
- claim/evidence needs
- sample decision criteria

## Round 4 — Delivery

1. Deliverables: microsite, PDF, email-ready summary, or all three?
2. Should the email be drafted only or sent after approval?
3. Recipients, sender/contact, subject line, and deadline
4. Public staging slug and confidentiality/noindex requirement
5. Available imagery; if none, ask whether to use verified public brand imagery
   as a clearly labelled reference or generate concept imagery

Output:

- delivery checklist
- approval owner
- final recipient list

## Evidence ledger

Tag every material statement:

- `customer-provided` — supplied by the operator/customer
- `brand-verified` — verified on the brand's current public materials
- `PLX-verified` — documented PLX capability or credential
- `proposal` — strategic or product-development direction
- `unknown` — requires customer confirmation

Hard stops:

- no invented price, volume, capacity, timing, certification, or efficacy
- no finished-formula claim supported only by ingredient evidence
- no proprietary formula/process language without disclosure authority
- no concept image presented as an approved product

## Ready-to-build gate

Begin once these are known:

- brand and meeting audience
- desired decisions
- at least one valid source
- concepts or business lanes in scope
- confidentiality status
- requested output and deadline

Everything else may remain an explicit assumption or open question.
